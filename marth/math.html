B. Evaluation and Operations on Functions

Solve the following. Show solutions.

12Given f(x) = 2x² − 3x + 5, find f(−4).
Substitute −4 for every x in the function.
f(−4) = 2(−4)² − 3(−4) + 5 = 2(16) + 12 + 5 = 49.
Answer: 49
13Given f(x) = 2x + 3 and g(x) = x² − 4, find (f + g)(3a).
First write (f + g)(x) = f(x) + g(x). Then substitute x = 3a.
(f + g)(3a) = f(3a) + g(3a)
= [2(3a) + 3] + [(3a)² − 4]
= 6a + 3 + 9a² − 4
= 9a² + 6a − 1.
14Given f(x) = 3x² + 2x − 1 and g(x) = x² − 4x + 5, find (f − g)(5).
Evaluate f(5) and g(5) separately, then subtract: f(5) − g(5).
f(5) = 3(5)² + 2(5) − 1 = 75 + 10 − 1 = 84.
g(5) = (5)² − 4(5) + 5 = 25 − 20 + 5 = 10.
(f − g)(5) = 84 − 10 = 74.
15Given f(x) = x + 2 and g(x) = 3x − 5, find (f · g)(x).
The dot means multiplication: (f · g)(x) = f(x)g(x).
(f · g)(x) = (x + 2)(3x − 5)
= 3x² − 5x + 6x − 10
= 3x² + x − 10.
16Given f(x) = x² − 36 and g(x) = x − 6, find (f ÷ g)(x). Simplify the result and state the restriction on x.
Write the quotient as a fraction, factor x² − 36 as a difference of squares, and remember that the original denominator cannot be zero.
(f ÷ g)(x) = x² − 36x − 6 = (x − 6)(x + 6)x − 6 = x + 6, with x ≠ 6.

C. Composite Functions

17Given f(x) = 2x + 1 and g(x) = x² − 3, find (f ∘ g)(x).
For (f ∘ g)(x), evaluate the inner function first: f(g(x)).
(f ∘ g)(x) = f(g(x)) = 2(x² − 3) + 1 = 2x² − 6 + 1 = 2x² − 5.
18Given f(x) = x² + 2 and g(x) = 3x − 1, find (g ∘ f)(−2).
Since (g ∘ f)(−2) = g(f(−2)), find f(−2) first.
f(−2) = (−2)² + 2 = 6.
g(6) = 3(6) − 1 = 17.
Answer: 17

D. Further Evaluation

19Given F(x) = 2x² − 3, find and simplify: F(a+h) − F(a)h
Find F(a+h) and F(a) first. Expand (a+h)², subtract carefully, then factor out h.
F(a+h) = 2(a+h)² − 3 = 2a² + 4ah + 2h² − 3.
F(a) = 2a² − 3.
F(a+h) − F(a)h = 4ah + 2h²h = h(4a + 2h)h = 4a + 2h, where h ≠ 0.
20A student buys notebooks that cost ₱45 each. The total cost is modeled by C(n) = 45n, where n represents the number of notebooks purchased. Evaluate C(6) and explain what the answer means in the given situation.
Substitute 6 for n in C(n) = 45n. Then interpret the result as a total cost.
C(6) = 45(6) = ₱270.
This means that buying 6 notebooks costs a total of ₱270.
