Problem 1 – Finding an Angle

A triangular garden has side lengths 12 m, 15 m, and 20 m. Find the angle opposite the 20-m side. Round the answer to the nearest degree.

Given: a = 12 m, b = 15 m, c = 20 m

Find: ∠C

Hint

All three sides are known. Use the Law of Cosines:

c² = a² + b² − 2ab cos C

Isolate cos C, then use the inverse cosine function.

Solution

c² = a² + b² − 2ab cos C

20² = 12² + 15² − 2(12)(15) cos C

400 = 144 + 225 − 360 cos C

400 = 369 − 360 cos C

31 = −360 cos C

cos C = −31360

C = cos−1 (−31360)

C ≈ 94.94°

Final Answer: ∠C ≈ 95°

Problem 2 – Finding Side b

A triangular park has two sides measuring 10 m and 14 m. The included angle between these two sides is 60°. Find the length of side b, which is opposite the 60° angle. Round the answer to the nearest tenth of a meter.

Given: a = 10 m, c = 14 m, ∠B = 60°

Find: b

Hint

Two sides and their included angle are known. Use the Law of Cosines:

b² = a² + c² − 2ac cos B

After finding b², take the square root to determine b.

Solution

b² = a² + c² − 2ac cos B

b² = 10² + 14² − 2(10)(14) cos 60°

b² = 100 + 196 − 280(0.5)

b² = 296 − 140

b² = 156

b = √156

b ≈ 12.49

Final Answer: b ≈ 12.5 m
